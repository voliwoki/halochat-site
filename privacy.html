Halo Chat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Halo Chat is a messaging app for sharing chats, photos, videos, stories and moods with friends. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how to delete it. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your information, and there are no ads or tracking.

Who can use Halo Chat

You must be at least 13 years old, or older where the law in your country requires it. People aged 13 to 17 get stricter privacy settings by default. We ask for your birthday when you sign up only to check your age and apply those settings.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a password (stored securely by our sign-in provider; we never see it), your birthday, and when you accepted the Community Rules.
  • Profile: your name, username, and anything you choose to add: profile photo or avatar, bio, pronouns, status and birthday display.
  • Friends: your friends, the people you follow and who follow you, friend and message requests, and people you have blocked.
  • Messages: text, photos, videos, voice messages, stickers, GIFs and reactions you send, plus whether a message was delivered and read, and when someone is typing. You can turn off read receipts, typing and "last active" in Settings.
  • Stories and moods: photos and videos you post to your story, who viewed and reacted to them, and the mood you pick.
  • Location (off unless you turn it on): if you share your location on the map, your position is saved so the friends you choose can see it. You can share an approximate location instead, use ghost mode, or stop at any time.
  • Notifications: a push notification address for your phone, and your notification choices (which alerts you want, quiet hours, time zone).
  • Reports: if you report a person or content, we keep the report so it can be reviewed.

We do not collect your contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing history, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Camera, microphone and face filters

The camera and microphone are used only when you take a photo, record a video or record a voice message. Face filters work out where your face is so the filter can follow it. That happens only on your phone, while a filter is on. We never save or send any information about your face, and we never use it to identify you.

Who can see your information

  • Your name and username can be found by others so they can add you, unless you turn on "Hide me from search" in Settings.
  • Your profile details can be set to friends only.
  • Messages are seen only by the people in that chat.
  • Stories are seen only by the audience you choose, for 24 hours.
  • Your mood is seen only by the friends you choose.
  • Your location is seen only by the friends you choose, never by followers or strangers.
  • If you block someone, they can no longer see your profile, stories, mood or location, or message you.

These rules are enforced on our servers, not only in the app.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase stores accounts, messages, photos and other app data, and runs our server code. Data is stored in Google data centres in Australia and the United States.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to your iPhone. By default, notifications do not show message text.
  • GIPHY provides GIFs. When you search for a GIF, your search words are sent to GIPHY.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Stories are deleted after 24 hours.
  • Moods clear after 24 hours.
  • A shared location expires after 8 hours, or as soon as you stop sharing.
  • Messages and their photos, videos and voice messages are kept until your account is deleted. A photo sent as "View once" can't be opened again in the app after it has been viewed, but the file stays on our servers until the sender's account is deleted.
  • Everything else is kept until you delete your account.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Settings > Download my data gives you a copy of your information.
  • Delete your account: Settings > Delete my account permanently removes your account, profile, stories, mood, location and one-to-one chats, with their photos and voice messages. In group chats, your old messages stay but show as from a deleted account. Reports you made are kept so they can still be reviewed.
  • Privacy settings: choose who can see your profile details, stories, mood and location, who can message you or send you friend requests, and which notifications you get.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules on our servers. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app about important changes.
